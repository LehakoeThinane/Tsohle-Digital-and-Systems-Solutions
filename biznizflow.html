The Problem We Solved

Most South African small and medium businesses run on disconnected tools — spreadsheets for inventory, separate apps for sales, different systems for accounting. This creates data silos, manual data entry, and operational chaos.

Our Solution: Unified Operational Intelligence

BiznizFlowPilot treats sales and operations as parts of one system, not separate departments. When a lead closes in the CRM, your ERP already knows about it. When inventory moves, your finance team sees it instantly. No manual data entry. No synchronization delays.

CRM Side: Lead to Close

The CRM handles your complete customer lifecycle with enforced workflows and intelligent automation.

  • Lead Pipeline Management
    Enforced state transitions from New → Contacted → Qualified → Won/Lost with full audit trails
  • Customer Relationship Tracking
    Complete contact history, interaction logs, and relationship analytics in one view
  • Task Management
    Assignment, priorities, due dates with role-based access control and status updates
  • Automated Follow-Ups
    Idle lead detection automatically triggers follow-up tasks via Celery scheduled jobs
  • CRM → ERP Handoff
    When a lead is marked Won, a "Create Sales Order" action appears and pre-fills with customer data
  • Activity Analytics
    Real-time visibility into team performance, conversion rates, and pipeline health

ERP Side: Operations to Finance

The ERP covers procurement, fulfillment, finance, HR, and inventory with state machine workflows and automated accounting.

  • Inventory Management
    Multi-location stock tracking with warehouse/shelf locations and computed available = quantity − reserved
  • Sales Orders
    Draft → confirmed → shipped → delivered state machine with stock reservation and auto-generated SO numbers
  • Purchase Orders
    Draft → sent → received pipeline linked to suppliers with automated stock updates
  • VAT-Inclusive Invoicing
    Line items, discount support, PDF generation, and CSV export for accounting systems
  • Finance Module
    Expense tracking by category, P&L summary, and South African tax compliance
  • HR Infrastructure
    Leave management with approval workflows, payroll generation, and payslip records

The Engineering Differentiator

What makes BiznizFlowPilot different isn't the features — it's the architecture. This is a production-grade system built on principles most agencies never touch.

Event-Driven Backbone

Every significant action (lead created, order shipped, invoice issued, task completed) emits a structured event with full audit trail. This creates a complete operational history and enables intelligent automation.

Workflow Engine: Rule-based automation with trigger → conditions → actions logic. Create tasks, send emails, or call webhooks in response to any event.

Async Processing: Celery + Redis workers handle event dispatch, workflow execution, retry orchestration, and stale-work recovery. The system never blocks waiting for external services.
