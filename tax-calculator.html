The Problem We Solved

Employees and contractors in South Africa need to estimate their take-home pay after tax, but most calculators are clunky external tools that don't integrate with business websites.

Our Solution: WordPress-Native Tax Calculator

This plugin brings accurate South African tax calculations directly into your WordPress site with a simple shortcode. Your employees or clients can estimate their take-home pay without leaving your platform.

Core Calculations

Accurate South African income tax computation based on current SARS tax tables and regulations.

  • PAYE Calculation
    Accurate South African income tax computation for annual or monthly salaries
  • UIF with Monthly Cap
    Unemployment Insurance Fund calculation respecting the R177.12 monthly cap
  • Medical Tax Credits
    Automatic calculation of medical aid tax credits based on dependents
  • Take-Home Pay Summary
    Clear breakdown showing gross income, deductions, and net take-home amount

WordPress Integration Features

Built as a native WordPress plugin with full integration into your site's ecosystem.

  • Simple Shortcode
    Install with [sa_tax_calculator] on any page or post
  • Brand Customization
    Match your company colors from the WordPress settings panel
  • PDF Generation
    Generate and download tax summary reports as PDFs
  • Media Library Integration
    Option to save PDFs directly to WordPress Media Library for record-keeping

Installation & Usage

Incredibly simple to deploy — just install the plugin and add the shortcode to any page.

[sa_tax_calculator]

That's it. The calculator will appear on your page with your branded colors and full functionality.
